# Privacy Policy — F.B. Sponsored/Ad Post Blocker

Last updated: 13 August 2026

## Summary

This extension collects nothing, transmits nothing, and contacts no server.

## What it does

The extension runs only on facebook.com. It reads the structure of the feed
page you already have open in order to recognise sponsored posts, "Suggested
for you" posts, and posts from Pages or Groups you do not follow, and then
hides those posts from view.

This happens entirely inside your browser, on your own device.

## Data collected

None.

The extension makes no network requests of any kind. It contains no analytics,
no tracking, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no advertising code. There
is no server associated with it, so there is nowhere for data to be sent even
in principle.

Specifically, it does not collect, store, or transmit:

- personally identifiable information
- health, financial, or location information
- authentication information, passwords, or cookies
- personal communications, messages, or posts
- your Facebook account details, friends, or activity
- browsing history or web activity
- keystrokes, clicks, or other user activity

## Data stored on your device

The extension stores four settings using your browser's local extension
storage:

- whether to hide sponsored posts
- whether to hide "Suggested for you" posts
- whether to hide posts from Pages/Groups you do not follow
- whether hidden posts collapse to a small placeholder or are removed entirely

These are on/off values only. They never leave your device, are not synced
between browsers, and are deleted when you uninstall the extension.

A count of how many posts have been hidden is shown on the toolbar icon. It is
held in memory for the current tab, is never written down or sent anywhere, and
resets when the page reloads.

## Permissions

- **Access to facebook.com** — required to read the feed and hide posts in it.
  This is the only site the extension can access. It reads page structure to
  identify posts; it does not read your messages, account details, or the
  content of posts, and does not transmit anything it sees.
- **Storage** — required to remember the four settings above between page
  loads.

No other permissions are requested. The extension does not request access to
your browsing history, your tabs in general, or any other website.

## Third parties

There are none. No data is shared, sold, or disclosed to anyone, because none
is collected.

## Remote code

The extension executes only the code contained in its published package. It
does not download, fetch, or evaluate code from any remote source.
