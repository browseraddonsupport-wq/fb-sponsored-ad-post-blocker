Almost ready

One permission left. This extension can't read your Facebook feed — and so can't hide anything in it — until you allow it to run on facebook.com.

Your browser will ask you to confirm. The extension requests access to facebook.com only, stores your settings on this device, and sends nothing anywhere.

You're all set. Sponsored posts will be hidden from your feed.

Open Facebook

Click the toolbar icon any time to change what gets hidden, or to see how many posts have been hidden on the current tab.

What it hides, by default

  • Sponsored posts and ads
  • "Suggested for you" posts
  • Posts from Pages and Groups you don't follow

Each is a checkbox in the toolbar popup. If your feed looks too sparse, "Pages and Groups you don't follow" is the broadest and the first one to turn off.
